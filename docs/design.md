# Visual design

flit's own look: quiet, dark, crisp. It continues what worked in Resound (custom-drawn surfaces, rounded components, small deliberate animations) with its own palette and mark. Code: `internal/ui/theme.go` (tokens), `widgets.go` (components), `feather.go` (the mark).

## Colour

Neutral greys without a blue tint, white and grey type, and **one accent**.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ColBase` | `#1C1C1E` | window background, the notch |
| `ColSidebar` | `#222224` | navigation column, the window's title bar (DWM caption colour) |
| `ColCard` / `ColCardHover` | `#28282B` / `#303034` | grouped content, hovered rows |
| `ColControl` / `ColControlHi` | `#3A3A3E` / `#48484D` | buttons, tracks, unselected segments; hovered |
| `ColDivider` / `ColEdge` | `#343438` / `#3C3C40` | lines; outlines of floating surfaces, window border |
| `ColText` | `#F4F4F6` | text, primary buttons, the "on" state of switches, progress |
| `ColMuted` / `ColFaint` | `#A3A3A9` / `#707076` | secondary text; captions and disabled |
| `ColInk` | `#1C1C1E` | text on white |
| **`ColLive`** | **`#F03C52`** | **only** what is live: the recording dot (tray, notch, window), live levels, and the soft light in the corner where the dot is |

The accent is a berry-tinted red between Resound's `#F2416F` and a clean red. Every other state (detected, ready, progress, selection) is expressed in white and grey: filled vs. hollow, bright vs. muted. Surfaces never use literal colours, only these tokens.

## Type and icons

- Segoe UI: 13 px body, 13 px semibold for emphasis and buttons, 11.5 px small, 11 px semibold upper-case captions, 15 px semibold card titles, 22 px semibold page titles. DirectWrite, grayscale anti-aliasing.
- Icons everywhere in the window and the tray panel (navigation, buttons, links, caption buttons, rows, the player): [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons) 3.49.0, outline with a 2-unit stroke and round caps and joins; filled for play, pause and the record dot. Embedded SVGs drawn as Direct2D polygons in the widget's colour, crisp at every scale ([ADR 0008](decisions/0008-tabler-icons.md), `internal/ui/icon.go`). Sizes: navigation 18 px, buttons 16, links 15, row icons 18, chevrons 14, round buttons 12. A recording in a list is `file-text`, a meeting `calendar-event`, both in white.

## Shape and motion

- Radii: cards 10 px, rows 8 px, buttons and segmented controls fully rounded (pill), the notch a capsule hanging from the screen edge.
- Primary buttons are white with dark text; secondary ones grey. At most one primary per group.
- Motion is short and purposeful: the notch drops in from the screen's edge (250 ms) and changes shape (215 ms) with a little overshoot that settles, a playful bounce rather than a wobble (`EaseOutBack`, about 8% past its place dropping in and 4% changing shape), its content fading in on an S-curve from 30% to 85% of the change; it leaves quickly and smoothly (200 ms, ease-in); live dots breathe (1.4 s period); a recording's player pill slides up from the window's bottom edge when its page opens (260 ms, ease-out) and back down when it is left (260 ms, ease-in); the wheel scrolls smoothly (each step eases out with a 50 ms time constant, at the system timer's ~64 Hz, offsets rounded to whole pixels so text stays crisp). Nothing animates while nothing changes; the window repaints at 20 fps only while something live is on screen. What a frame costs: [research/window-rendering.md](research/window-rendering.md).
- **The live glow**: where the recording dot is, a soft light of `ColLive` sits in the corner as if a blurred dot were just outside the surface: the tray panel's top left, and the window's bottom left behind the sidebar's "Recording 12:34". It reaches 150×140 px, is 20% opaque at its centre with a Gaussian falloff that reaches zero at its edge, is dithered against banding, and breathes with the dot (75–100% opacity). The idea is Resound's panel glow ([references/resound.md](references/resound.md#the-panel-glow)); the code is flit's (`internal/ui/glow.go`, drawn as a cached bitmap through `Canvas.Bitmap`). The tray panel's 1 px border is painted by flit (Windows' own is turned off) so the light reaches it: near the lit corner it turns towards a light red; otherwise it is `ColEdge`.

## The mark: a feather

The source is **`assets/flit.svg`** (one outline, viewBox 152×157): a quill leaning right with the barbs notched into its edge and a slit along the spine, filled white fading to 80% towards the tip. It is built into the exe (`assets/assets.go`, `go:embed`), and `internal/ui/feather.go` reads its path (`ParseSVGPath`: M/L/H/V/C/Q/Z) and its linear gradient, fits the path into a unit square (0.09–0.91 in height, centred) and draws it with flit's rasterizer (`vector.go`, curves flattened finely enough for 256 px) or as Direct2D polygons. Editing the SVG changes the tray icon and the window's mark on the next build.

The tray icon and the sidebar use the bare feather in flat white (or dark ink on a light taskbar), which stays crisper small. The app icon is its own artwork, `assets/logo.png` (table below). The SVG's 2-unit white stroke is left out (under 1% of the width).

After changing `assets/logo.png`, regenerate the exe's icon: `go run ./cmd/flitdev icon` writes `assets/flit.ico` with 18 sizes from 16 to 256 px, scaled from the logo, (every size Windows asks for between 100% and 400% scaling, so nothing gets scaled and blurred), which `go-winres` embeds at build time. The window's own big and small icons are scaled from the logo at run time to the exact size for the window's DPI.

Uses:

| Where | Form |
|---|---|
| App icon (exe, Start Menu, Installed apps, window) | **`assets/logo.png`** (256×256): the shaded white feather on a dark rounded tile with a bevelled lighter rim. Scaled to each size with a Catmull-Rom filter in premultiplied colour, widened when shrinking (`ui.AppIcon`) |
| Tray | the bare feather in white (or dark ink on a light taskbar); states as badges: hollow = meeting detected, **red dot = recording**, filled = transcript ready; while transcribing, the feather fills up from the bottom; faded = error |
| Window sidebar | white feather beside the "flit" wordmark |



## The window

A native Windows window (resizing, snapping, the system menu and accessibility of the frame all standard) with a dark frame via DWM (`DWMWA_USE_IMMERSIVE_DARK_MODE`, border colour). The title bar is folded into the client area ([ADR 0005](decisions/0005-drawn-caption-buttons.md)): the window is dragged by the top 32 px wherever nothing is clickable, and flit draws **minimise** and **close** in the top right corner (46×32 each, Tabler’s minus and x in the muted grey; hover lifts minimise to the card colour and turns close flit's red with a white glyph). No maximise button; double-click on the top strip maximises. Over the page, those 32 px are a bar in the page colour the buttons sit on: content scrolls under it (hidden and not clickable there), with no line at its edge. The sidebar stays one tone to the top. The client area is custom-drawn:

- **Sidebar** (208 px): the mark, navigation (Welcome until setup is done, Home, Recordings, Calendar, Audio, Settings), and a status line at the bottom; while a newer release is published, an **update pill** sits above the debug lines (or the status line), left-aligned with their text: 20 px tall (about half a navigation row), fully rounded, white (90% while hovered, 78% pressed), a 6 px dot in `ColInk` 10 px from its left end and "Update available" in the caption font (11 px semibold) in `ColInk`, as wide as its content; a click opens the Update page. White rather than the red, which stays for live; with the live dot while recording and the live glow in the sidebar's bottom left corner behind it. Above the status line's divider, two quiet lines: the speech model (loaded, or the one the next transcription loads) and what it uses ("1.12 GB · 34% CPU", or "Not loaded"), counting every engine worker running: the one after a meeting and the live transcription's own during one. While something is being transcribed (after a meeting, finding speakers, or as it goes during one), small **core squares** sit to the right of the two lines: one per core the engine keeps busy (counted from its threads' CPU time over the last second, including threads its pool replaced meanwhile: [research/engine-cores.md](research/engine-cores.md)), busiest first, at least the live worker's threads so they stay up, empty, between its chunks, at most eight, in a grid two high, 10 px with 2 px corners and 3 px apart, each filled from the top by how busy its core is (white at 43% on a faint white square). The busiest sit at the top right, the rest fill column by column leftwards; at rest a 2×2 shows in full and the two columns to its left fade out (45%, 18%); while the lines are hovered, all of them show in full. They highlight on hover and open the **Debug** page: the engine (model, worker pid or pids, memory, CPU, cores engaged, VAD model, transcribe.cpp version, current job), flit itself (memory, CPU, Go heap and goroutines, uptime) and the log's latest 80 lines in Consolas, with links to the log file and data folder. Figures are sampled at most once a second; the window repaints every 2 s (every second while the core squares show; 1 s on the Debug page) only while it is open. The cores are read off the UI thread: one `NtQuerySystemInformation` call lists every process's threads with their CPU times, about 5 ms (a Toolhelp snapshot with `GetThreadTimes` per thread took about 60 ms), so about half a percent of one core while transcribing.
- **Page** (scrolls; at most 720 px wide, 32 px margins): the title, then cards. Buttons line up on the left under what they act on; nothing sits in the top band beside the window's caption buttons.
- **Welcome** (until setup is done): the title and a sentence about flit, a card with the meeting language and Start with Windows (the transcription switches, on and Balanced by default, are in Settings only, to keep it short), then **Speech models**: a **download card** per model the settings need (the speech model, voice detection, and the speaker model when speakers are told apart), Done at the end. They download at the same time, each model behind its own lock so one is never fetched twice at once. A download card is a card the width of the page, 58 px tall, whose background fills from the left in the hover shade as the model comes down: the name in semibold, under it what the model is for and how much of it is here ("Speech model · 320 MB of 706 MB"), and one small grey figure on the right: the percentage, or Ready, Waiting, Failed. Home shows the cards of models coming down, and the transcription after a meeting on the same card ("Transcribing Weekly sync", when and how long, "at most 50% of the processor" when limited, and the percentage), its background filling as it goes. Everywhere else there is one number too: the status says what is downloading ("Downloading Parakeet Ultra", "Downloading 3 models") and the notch, the tray panel and the sidebar's status line add the percentage of all of it.
- **Home**: the title, then one sentence saying what flit is doing with the main action beside it as a large pill: Start recording; while recording **Cancel** (throws the recording away, after a second click) and **Stop and transcribe**; while a meeting is detected Not now / Transcribe. A card follows only when something is live (a recording's voice meter, below; a transcription's progress). Then **Latest recordings** (the newest four) with a link to **All recordings**.
- **Recordings**: how many and how much is on disk, Open folder and Free up space (to Settings), a **search** field (magnifier, "Search recordings", × to clear) with **By name** / **Also in transcripts** beside it, then every recording, or those that match. The list filters while typing; every word must match, ignoring case. By name means the title and the date ("zoom 20 nov"); also in transcripts adds what was said, and a recording found by its words shows the line they are on ("Anna: … zwei offene Punkte …") instead of its first words. A line under the field says how many match. Esc in the field goes back to what was searched before.
- A recording in progress is only on Home, in its recording card: Latest recordings, the Recordings list, its count and the search leave it out until it stops; then it joins them, being transcribed.
- Home, Recordings and the tray panel use the same **recording row**: the document icon (18 px, white; the live red dot while recording) in an icon column, centred on the title and facts lines; the title with the state and a chevron on its line; a line of facts (when · length · words · speakers) and, for a transcript, its first words in the faint grey; every line ends at the chevron's right edge; a click opens it. A row can carry round icon buttons on its right instead of the chevron (the panel's copy). Padding: 12 px at the sides, 8 px above and below.
- **Recording and meeting rows share their columns**: the same icon column (a meeting's is the calendar icon), and a meeting's start and end times begin where a recording's title and date do, on the same two lines (bold start over the end, as the bold title over the date). The meeting's title follows after a 40 px time column. So on Home, the Calendar and Recordings, everything lines up. The tray panel shows no row icons: there, the times and the transcript's title both start at the row's padding.
- **A recording**: back link; the **title, renamed in place** (hover shows a box and a pencil; click, type, Enter saves, Esc cancels, empty goes back to the default name; the transcripts' header line follows); facts and state; Copy, Copy as Markdown, Open folder, Transcribe again, left-aligned; **Speakers** (each with a small round play button that plays that person's clearest moments, up to three stretches of up to 8 s where nobody else talks, from their own track, with the bar saying whose they are; "Analyze speakers again" (a link with the redo icon beside the Speakers caption) runs diarization on the kept audio again); the **transcript** as speaker paragraphs (name, time, text; 6 px above and below each, so more of the conversation fits), the one playing marked with the hover colour and a thin white bar on its left. The text is **selectable** with the pointer (an I-beam over it), across paragraphs: a double click selects a word (letters and digits, with the hyphens and apostrophes inside it: "Release-Plan", "geht's"), a triple click the whole paragraph (counted within Windows' double-click time and distance); dragging near the top or bottom of the page's view (28 px inside the title bar, or above the docked player) scrolls it, faster the further out, and the selection follows what comes under the pointer, as it does after a wheel turn mid-drag; every paragraph's text stays selectable while scrolled out of view, so a selection keeps both its ends however far it reaches. The selection is a soft white band under the glyphs, Ctrl+C copies it (the selected part of each paragraph, joined by blank lines), Esc or a click elsewhere lets go. So a click no longer plays a paragraph; instead, the pointer on a paragraph shows two quiet round buttons at the right of its speaker line: **play** (that paragraph alone, both tracks, from just before it starts to just after it ends; pause while it plays, and it stays shown meanwhile) and **copy** (its text; a check mark for 1.5 s). Where characters are comes from DirectWrite's line and cluster metrics, turned into caret stops in Go (`TextLines`, `internal/ui/textsel.go`): its hit-testing calls take floats in registers, which flit's cgo-free calls can't pass. In the transcript card's top right, in a row of its own, is a **search** field ("Search transcript", 220 px, fully rounded, × to clear); while it holds words, every match in the transcript is red, the one shown also underlined, and "3 of 12" with up and down arrows (chevron icon buttons) sits on its left, going through the results round the ends ("No results" when there are none). Typing shows the first result and every arrow the next or previous: the page scrolls smoothly so its paragraph is a third of the way down the view. While searching, the field and its arrows stay at the top of the view as the transcript scrolls past (until the card's end), on a pill in the card's hover shade without an outline, which takes the clicks over the text beneath; around the pill only, a halo of the card colour at 76% at its edge fades out over 24 px in every direction, following its rounded shape, so the text scrolling beneath recedes around it. The field is fully rounded, to sit in the pill. Matching ignores case and finds the words as typed, as one phrase; the search is forgotten when the page is left. And **Files**: delete the audio (keeps the transcript) or the whole recording, each after a second click within 5 s ("Click again to …", red). While the recording has audio, the **player** floats in a pill at the bottom of the page area, always in reach: 16 px above the window's edge, snug around the controls (4 px above and below, 14 px right), in the card colour with no outline. The controls line up with the page column, so the play button's left edge is on the content's left edge and the time ends on its right edge; the pill reaches out past them by its padding. On the left that padding is half of what the pill is taller than the round play button (11 px), so the button sits concentric with the pill's rounded end. It doesn't scroll; the page goes on beneath it and can scroll far enough for its end to clear it. Behind it, a backdrop of the page colour at 63% from the pill's top edge down fades out over the 56 px above, so the page dissolves behind the pill. A click on the pill never reaches the page under it. It slides up out of the bottom edge when the page opens and back down into it when the page is left (260 ms; while leaving it takes no clicks). The player: round white play/pause, a thin white progress bar on the control grey, a knob that grows on hover and while held, the time on the right. A click on the bar jumps there; dragging along it (**scrubbing**) moves the knob and the time with the pointer, even outside the window, and playback goes on from where it is let go. Audio keeps playing while the window is minimised and stops when it closes or the page changes.
- **Calendar** (with a calendar link set): the feed's name and when it was updated, Refresh and Settings; then the meetings of today and the next seven days under a caption per day ("Today", "Tomorrow", then "Monday, 23 November"). A **meeting row** (columns shared with recording rows, above): the calendar icon, start over end in a narrow column (bold start), the title with the place under it (the words, or the meeting app's name when the place is only a link), and on the right **Join** (opens the link) or **Recording** (when one was made of it), with **Record** (white dot) appearing to their left while the pointer is on the row, which records now for that meeting; the meeting going on now has a thin white bar on its left; past ones are greyed. Without a link: a sentence about it and "Add a calendar". A warning card only after a day of failed refreshes.
- **Home** shows **Upcoming meetings** (the next two, "Tomorrow · Zoom" when not today) above Latest recordings, with a link to the Calendar.
- **A recording** matched to a meeting shows it under the facts ("From your calendar · Tue 24 Nov, 14:00–15:00 · Zoom", with "Not this meeting"); when meetings overlapped, a card asks **Which meeting was this?** with the candidates and "None of these".
- **Settings → Calendar**: the subscription link in a full-width field, Test (loads it once and says how many events and meetings it found, or what went wrong) and Remove.
- **Settings → Transcription** has **Transcribe during meetings** (on by default, [ADR 0009](decisions/0009-quiet-transcription-by-default.md)); when on, **Background load** (Light, Balanced, Faster: at most 15, 25, 40% of the processor, at low priority). Below it, **Limit the transcription after a meeting** (on by default, Balanced); when on, **Load** (Light, Balanced, Faster: at most 25, 50, 75% of the processor, at low priority, with as many engine threads; its detail says how many), so the PC doesn't spin up: Balanced made a 5-minute meeting take 24 s instead of 20 ([research/engine-cores.md](research/engine-cores.md)). While recording with it on, Home's recording card adds "Transcribing as it goes: up to 12:40".
- **Update** (reached from the sidebar's update pill, only while a newer release is known, [ADR 0010](decisions/0010-updates-from-github-releases.md)): the title, a sentence ("flit 0.2.0 is available; this is 0.1.0. Updating restarts flit; your recordings, transcripts, models and settings stay as they are."), a download card for the new version (as the models': "New", then filling with the percentage and "Downloading · 11 MB of 30 MB", then Ready or Installing), and under it **Update now** (primary, large, download icon) and **What's new** (opens the release page); Cancel while downloading. During a recording the downloaded update waits ("Restart and update", disabled, with a line saying it follows the recording) and starts by itself when the recording is stopped. Errors on a card below. Settings → App has *Check for updates* (its detail says what the last check found) and a *Check now* link.
- **Settings** ends with **Free up space and uninstall**: audio older than 30 days, all audio of transcribed recordings, all recordings and transcripts, the downloaded speech models (each with what it frees, counted when the page opens; recordings being recorded, transcribed or analysed are left alone), each asking for a second click; and, for the installed copy, **Uninstall…**, which opens a popover (below).
- **The uninstall popover**: in the middle of the window, on the card colour with a 1 px edge and a soft shadow, while the rest dims to 55% black and takes no clicks (it fades in over 160 ms; Esc or Cancel closes it, and so does leaving the page). "Uninstall flit", what it removes, and a choice: **Keep my recordings** (the default: recordings, transcripts and settings stay in the data folder, and a new install picks them up) or **Delete everything** (recordings, transcripts, speech models and settings), each with the data folder's size. The button follows the choice: Uninstall (primary), or Uninstall and delete (red). It starts the installed exe (from the temp folder) as `--uninstall --confirmed` (plus `--delete-data`), which quits flit, stops whatever still runs from the install folder (flit stuck quitting, an engine worker left over holding the models open), removes its entries and autostart, deletes the data folder at once when asked (its own log closed first, trying again for up to 10 s while a file is still held), and leaves the program folder, with itself in it, to a hidden cmd.exe that removes it moments after it exits (five tries a second apart), with whatever of the data folder was left. Keeping the recordings keeps the settings and the downloaded speech models too; the engine's DLLs unpacked into the data folder go with the program. It deletes only a data folder that holds flit's `config.json` or `sessions`. (Until 0.8.7 nothing was deleted: Go quoted the paths for cmd.exe as `\"…\"`, which cmd doesn't understand; `TestDeleteLater` and `TestUninstallDeletesEverything` now check it.) Started from Windows' Installed apps, the uninstaller asks with a Windows dialog and keeps the data.
- **The voice meter** (Home's recording card, the tray panel while recording): a box in the page colour with 10 px corners holding "You" and "Meeting". On Home it is **stacked**: two 26 px rows in a narrow box (as wide as the labels and bars need) on the right of the card, beside the title and the sentence about what is recorded; when the window is too narrow to leave the text 280 px, it goes under the text, **side by side**: one 40 px row across the card, the two halves split by a thin divider. In the tray panel it is side by side, under Stop and transcribe and Cancel. Each label is followed by a small curve of 9 bars: 4 px wide, 3 px apart, rounded, centred, 3 px tall at rest and up to 18 px while that side speaks, dimmer while quiet (45% white) and brighter with the voice. The bars are the voice range of each track in 9 bands (400–4000 Hz, narrower low than high, the higher bands lifted 6 dB per octave and each measured from 6 dB above its own noise floor, so speech fills the middle of the curve and steady hiss doesn't move it: [research/voice-meter-bands.md](research/voice-meter-bands.md)), measured about 31 times a second; the shown bars ease towards each reading (time constants 45 ms up, 130 ms down) and repaint about 60 times a second on their own: only their pixels, drawn from Go, not the window (`ui.VoiceMeter`, `Ctx.DrawLive`; costs in [research/window-rendering.md](research/window-rendering.md#live-regions)). The look follows Handy's recording overlay ([references/handy.md](references/handy.md#the-recording-overlays-waveform)).
- **Audio** page: every microphone and output as a radio choice with its live level bar (meters run only while the page is on screen), and the apps using a microphone right now, as detection sees them.

## The notch while recording and after

- **Meeting detected**: with "Record meetings automatically" on (the default), the notch says "<app> meeting detected · recording starts automatically" with Record now and Cancel, and a lighter band fills the capsule from left to right over 5 seconds; when it is full, recording starts. Cancel leaves this meeting out (until it ends). Off: Transcribe / Not now, as before, hiding after 30 s.
- **Recording**: the full notch (meeting, clock, levels and Stop, all at once: opening it is the only step) shows when recording starts, then collapses after 3 s without the pointer on it to a 46×11 px capsule at the top edge. The collapsed capsule lets clicks through (`WS_EX_TRANSPARENT`; the cursor is polled instead of hit-tested), so it never blocks a tab or title bar beneath it. Resting the pointer on it for 1 s expands it again; a soft glow grows during that wait so the user sees it is coming. Colour cues in the collapsed capsule: a pulsing red dot in the capsule's left end while recording, with the microphone's level as a bar across the rest of its width (a silent mic is visible at a glance); a white fill growing left to right while transcribing; a check mark and "Finished" (8 px semibold, white; the capsule as tall as ever and just wide enough for them) when a transcript waits. The cue keeps the capsule's resting size and stays centred on it: when the capsule swells under the pointer or changes size, it grows around the cue.
- **After the meeting**: the notch stays (collapsed when not hovered) through transcription and until the user handles the result: the expanded "Transcript ready" offers **Copy** (copies and closes) and **Close**; its title and word count are a link (underlined while the pointer is on them) that opens the recording's page in the window and closes the notch, which counts as handling it, as copying does. It doesn't time out.

## Speakers and names

A recording's page has a **Speakers** card between its actions and the transcript: one row per speaker (Me first, then Speaker 1, 2, … in order of first words), each with a stretch of what they said (their longest segment, cut to about twelve words), how long they spoke and when they first did, and a **name field** on the right. The field shows the given name, or the default ("Me", "Speaker 1") as a faint placeholder; a click selects all of it so typing replaces it; Enter, clicking elsewhere or closing the window saves; Esc cancels. Below the card: a hint ("Two speakers with the same name read as one person"), the state of a running search, or why it failed, and **Find speakers (again)** when the meeting's track is still there.

The text field (`internal/ui/textfield.go`) is flit's only text input so far: one line, drawn like the other controls (the control colour, lighter while hovered or edited, a thin white line under it while editing, a blinking caret, the selection as a faint white band); long text scrolls to keep the caret in view.

## The tray panel

A right-click on the tray icon opens a rounded popover beside the taskbar (above a bottom taskbar, below a top one, beside a side one), in the window's style on the sidebar colour, closing like a menu (focus lost, Esc, or after an action). It keeps to what matters, without explanations (the window has those): the state and its one action (recording: live dot, title, clock, Stop and transcribe and **Cancel** (which throws the recording away after a second click, "Click again", as on Home), then the voice meter; detected meeting: Record now or Transcribe, Cancel or Not now; transcribing: progress; idle: Start recording); with a calendar, the next two meetings as meeting rows with small round icon buttons to record (white dot, not while recording) and to join (when it has a link), a click on the row opening the Calendar; the latest transcript as a recording row with a round copy button like the join button, a click on the row opening it in the window; both kinds of row light up with the hover colour under the pointer; the meeting language; and at the bottom the flit mark and name with "Click to open the app" on the left, Quit on the right (neutral, red while hovered). Its height fits its content. While recording, its top left corner glows (the live glow, above).

Pages are built from state on every change (`internal/ui/view.go`): a layout pass records drawing and click targets together, so what is drawn and what is clickable can't disagree.
